# Future You

The `/goals` dashboard follows the [Future You viewport](https://www.figma.com/design/fxGXlNob258j5CekcAFxIH/SEA-x-OpenAI-Codex-Hackathon-2026-Brainstorm?node-id=67-1974) and the [full goals frame](https://www.figma.com/design/fxGXlNob258j5CekcAFxIH/SEA-x-OpenAI-Codex-Hackathon-2026-Brainstorm?node-id=54-1816). Artwork exported from the viewport on 2026-11-21 lives in `public/future-you`.

The dashboard takes its large balance and funding ring from the Brim examples and its color-coded cards from the Slava widget studies in `UI-design-direction/`. The overview sums life-goal savings, including personal goals. The ring measures how much of the combined targets is covered, counting each goal up to its own target. The emergency reserve is separate. The hero uses the supplied `doraemon assets/nobita-with-future-nobita-movie-version.webp`.

The four life goals and separate emergency reserve use synthetic figures from the supplied dashboard export and Nobita × Doraemon demo financial model. Each card opens its funding chart, remaining balance, monthly contribution and planning horizon. Add goal creates a personal goal in the Life goals section. In shared demo mode, confirmed plans are saved through FastAPI into the same MongoDB profile used by Future Talk and the Companion. Standalone mode retains browser storage. The editor keeps unsaved changes private until Save succeeds.

Future Talk opens the saved-profile voice experience at `/future-you`. Future World opens the playable town at `/future-world`. Its four starter landmarks offer trip tickets at `/future-you/[goal]`, and each ticket links back to its landmark. The time-machine transition uses the character positions in Figma frame `54:1904`. The vault photograph and Doraemon face were isolated from the two user-supplied PNG exports.

Future World opens in town at `/future-world`. The guide, goal landmarks and furnished houses share one full-screen Three.js scene. Walk with the thumbstick, WASD or arrow keys, or tap a path to move through town. Approach a house and choose Enter house. Crossing its doorway returns you to the same doorstep in town. You can also tap Exit to town or press E near the door. Each interior shows its own saved goal, and other landmarks show their goal details when inspected. The existing `/future-world/town` link leads into the same town. The miniature house styling follows the [Spline reference](https://app.spline.design/file/650ef7f8-8e0d-4bf0-b5a5-136782cad041).
Future Talk opens the profile-based conversation. Future World opens a chooser for the four illustrated goals and takes the selected goal to `/future-you/[goal]`. The time-machine transition uses the character positions in Figma frame `54:1904`. The vault photograph and Doraemon face were isolated from the two user-supplied PNG exports.
