# Product context

Source: [Hackathon build ideas & solutions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nYO4LLQA7QZtbYPdawa4LRCryfyhW-Gx68bP92clmcw/edit), last modified 2026-11-15; read 2026-11-21.

## Vision and audience

An intent-based AI financial companion / digital relationship manager for Thai investors. The concept aims to help users understand their goals, build investing discipline, and reflect before decisions driven by panic, FOMO, or other emotional triggers. Success centers on long-term user outcomes and financial capability rather than transaction frequency.

The core journey is: know what you are investing for, build the habits to get there, and make better decisions along the way.

## Three pillars and candidate features

| Pillar | Purpose | Ideas in the source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Know Yourself | Understand financial goals in life | Future you / future world; timing nudges; adaptive risk profile |
| Invest with Discipline | Build healthy investing habits | Investor score; intentional friction vault; talking to future self; sub-portfolios; investing budget |
| Become a Smarter Investor | Make informed decisions | Self journal; holistic financial recommendations; conversational AI financial advisor |

## Intended behavior

- Learn users' investing habits and help them recognize cognitive biases and emotional triggers.
- Support habits such as consistent contributions, diversification, and reflection during volatility.
- Make an Financial Score transparent enough for users to understand strengths and areas to improve.
- Let users define goals and budgets, then show whether their strategy is ahead, on track, or behind those goals.
- Give contextual explanations that develop independent judgment and avoid overreliance on automated answers.
